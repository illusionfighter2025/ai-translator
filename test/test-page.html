Artificial Intelligence and the Modern Web

Artificial intelligence is rapidly changing how people interact with information online. Search engines, recommendation systems, and conversational assistants now shape much of what we read and discover every day.

Key idea: Large language models can translate and summarize web content in real time, making knowledge accessible across language barriers.

For travelers, students, and researchers, on-demand translation means a foreign news article or technical document is only one click away from being fully understood.

  • Full-page translation preserves layout while swapping text.
  • Selection translation gives instant answers for a single phrase.
  • Summaries compress long articles into a few key points.
